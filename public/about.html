About Valen's Visual Lab

Valen's Visual Lab is an educational open source platform dedicated to helping students and enthusiasts understand algorithms through interactive visualizations. Our mission is to make learning algorithms engaging and accessible for everyone.

Our Features

  • Interactive Visualizations: Step-by-step animations that illustrate how algorithms work.
  • Complexity Analysis: Tools to help users understand time and space complexity.
  • Diverse Algorithm Library: A wide range of algorithms from sorting and searching to graph algorithms.

Get Involved

We welcome contributions from the community! Whether you're a developer, educator, or student, there are many ways to get involved. Check out our GitHub repository for more information.
